English

OnceScan does not collect any data and sends nothing anywhere. Your scans go only where you put them.

What we collect

Nothing. OnceScan has no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not send your documents, photos or text to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data. The app makes no network requests of its own; the only connection is Apple's App Store handling purchases.

How your documents are processed

  • Scanning, straightening, clean-up, text recognition (OCR), reading e-invoice QR codes, automatic naming and sorting all run on your iPhone.
  • The camera is used only while you scan. Photos are chosen with Apple's photo picker; OnceScan only sees the photos you pick. OnceScan asks to add to your Photos only if you share a page as a photo and choose "Save Image".
  • Your scans, page images, recognized text, labels, notes and the original file of an imported PDF are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. iOS encrypts them. With "Strongest file protection" turned on in Settings, they can't be read at all while your iPhone is locked, not even by OnceScan. This covers the scans, pages and text the app stores. It does not cover small settings kept in the app's preferences (such as the labels remembered for each shop, and the names and tax IDs in "Who You Are"), nor PDFs already in your filing folder or its Old Versions folder.
  • If you fill in "Who You Are" in Settings (your name, company or tax ID), it stays in the app's settings on your iPhone and is only used to recognize the invoices you send out.
  • If you import a password-protected PDF, the password is used once to open it and is not stored; the unlocked copy is kept inside OnceScan like any other scan.
  • "Add to Calendar" opens Apple's new-event screen with the due date filled in. OnceScan does not read your calendar, and nothing is added unless you save it there.
  • Text you copy from a scan goes to the iPhone's clipboard. OnceScan keeps it on this device and lets it expire after a few minutes.

Saving to a folder (off unless you turn it on)

If you turn on "Save every scan as a PDF in a folder", OnceScan saves each scan as a PDF into the folder you chose in the Files app. If that folder is on your iPhone, the PDFs stay on your iPhone. If it is in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs those PDFs like any other file in iCloud Drive, under Apple's terms. If it is in another cloud drive app, that app handles them under its own terms. OnceScan itself never uploads anything.

When you edit the pages of a filed scan, OnceScan does not overwrite the PDF already in your folder (it may carry your notes or signature). The earlier PDF is moved, unchanged, into an "Old Versions" folder next to it, and the new pages are saved as a new PDF. OnceScan keeps up to 3 earlier versions of each scan there and removes older ones beyond that. You can delete them in the Files app at any time.

Filed PDFs and old versions are ordinary files: the Face ID lock in OnceScan does not protect them. Anyone who can open the Files app on your unlocked iPhone can open them, and the text inside them can be searched.

Sharing

Apart from your filing folder, a document leaves the app only when you share it yourself, for example to Mail, Messages or the Files app. What happens next is up to the app or service you choose.

Deleting your data

A scan you delete first goes to Recently Deleted, where it stays for 30 days so you can recover it. After 30 days it is deleted for good the next time you open OnceScan; you can also delete it for good right away in Recently Deleted. Deleting the app removes all of your scans inside OnceScan.

PDFs already saved to your filing folder, their old versions, and anything you shared are not deleted with the scan, unless you turn on "Delete filed PDFs too". They are ordinary files you can delete in the Files app.

One small number stays in your iPhone's Keychain: how many documents have been created, so the free allowance is not reset by reinstalling. It contains no document content and is never sent to us. Like other Keychain items, it is included in your encrypted iPhone backup and moves to a new iPhone that you set up from that backup.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your device backup may include OnceScan's data, under Apple's terms.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

If you email us

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message or attach. We use it only to answer you, keep it only as long as the conversation needs, and delete it when you ask. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), so Google processes these emails under its terms. The legal basis is answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in giving support (Art. 6(1)(f)).

If you are in the EU, the UK or a country with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you (which is only support emails), to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

Children

OnceScan does not collect data from anyone, including children.
