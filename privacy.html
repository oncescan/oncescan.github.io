English

OnceScan does not collect any data and sends nothing anywhere. Your scans go only where you put them.

What we collect

Nothing. OnceScan has no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not send your documents, photos or text to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive your data. The app makes no network requests of its own; the only connection is Apple's App Store handling purchases.

How your documents are processed

  • Scanning, straightening, clean-up, text recognition (OCR), automatic naming and sorting all run on your iPhone.
  • The camera is used only while you scan. Photos are chosen with Apple's photo picker; OnceScan only sees the photos you pick.
  • Your scans, page images and recognized text are stored on your iPhone, inside the app.

Automatic filing (off unless you turn it on)

If you turn on automatic filing, OnceScan saves each scan as a PDF into the folder you chose in the Files app. If that folder is on your iPhone, the PDFs stay on your iPhone. If it is in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs those PDFs like any other file in iCloud Drive, under Apple's terms. If it is in another cloud drive app, that app handles them under its own terms. OnceScan itself never uploads anything.

Sharing

Apart from your filing folder, a document leaves the app only when you share it yourself, for example to Mail, Messages or the Files app. What happens next is up to the app or service you choose.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app, or delete the app to remove all of your scans inside OnceScan. PDFs already saved to your filing folder, or shared elsewhere, are not deleted; they are ordinary files you can delete in the Files app. One small number stays in your iPhone's Keychain: how many documents have been created, so the free allowance is not reset by reinstalling. It contains no document content and never leaves your device.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your device backup may include OnceScan's data, under Apple's terms.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Children

OnceScan does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Bei einer anderen Cloud-App gelten deren Bedingungen. OnceScan selbst lädt nie etwas hoch.

Bereits abgelegte oder geteilte PDFs werden dabei nicht gelöscht; sie sind normale Dateien, die du in der Dateien-App löschen kannst. Nur eine Zahl bleibt im Schlüsselbund: wie viele Dokumente erstellt wurden, damit das Gratiskontingent bei einer Neuinstallation nicht zurückgesetzt wird. Sie enthält keine Inhalte und verlässt dein Gerät nie. Wenn du iCloud-Backup oder ein Backup am Computer nutzt, kann dieses Backup OnceScan-Daten enthalten (gemäß Apples Bedingungen).
